# STAR Behavioral Story Framework

> **Purpose:** Structural reference framework for building behavioral interview responses using Situation, Task, Action, and Result (STAR) with quantified outcomes. Version 1.0.0, 2026-09-07.

---

## 1. STAR Response Architecture

Structure behavioral interview responses using four distinct components:

1. **Situation (15% of Time):** Concise context setting. Define company stage, team scope, constraint, or challenge.
2. **Task (15% of Time):** Specific responsibility or objective assigned to you.
3. **Action (50% of Time):** Detailed technical or strategic steps you took. Highlight personal contribution, methodology, and decision logic.
4. **Result (20% of Time):** Quantified outcome, business impact, lesson learned, or key metric change.

---

## 2. Metric Anchoring Rules

Every STAR story must anchor its Result phase with at least one verifiable quantitative metric:

- **Revenue / Profit Impact:** Dollar value increase, growth percentage, cost savings.
- **Performance / Efficiency Gain:** Latency reduction percentage, pipeline throughput improvement, automation hours saved.
- **Scale / Volume:** User count reached, system uptime percentage, transaction volume processed.
- **Team / Organizational Growth:** Developers mentored, team retention rate, onboarding timeline reduction.

---

## 3. Behavioral Question Category Mapping

Map candidate stories to the core behavioral question categories:

| Category               | Primary Focus Area                                | Key Action Focus                                    |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Conflict & Resolution  | Navigating technical or strategic disagreements   | Stakeholder alignment, objective data evaluation    |
| Failure & Recovery     | Post-mortem analysis and system resilience        | Root-cause diagnosis, preventive safeguards         |
| Leadership & Influence | Driving outcomes without direct authority         | Technical direction, cross-functional collaboration |
| Technical Execution    | Solving complex engineering or product challenges | Architectural trade-offs, measurable delivery       |
