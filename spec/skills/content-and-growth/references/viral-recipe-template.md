# Viral Post Recipe Reference

A structural blueprint for contrarian anaphora social media posts. The subject changes per domain, but the underlying narrative mechanics stay fixed.

## Structural Blueprint

Work in three distinct phases:

1. **Gather the Essentials:** Identify the core topic, target audience, and primary outcome claimed.
2. **Draft Narrative Skeleton:** Propose the contrarian without list, 3 numbered core steps, and closer line.
3. **Refine & Polish:** Iterate on line pacing, tension, and closer options until publication ready.

---

## Narrative Mechanics Reference

### 1. Contrarian Anaphora Openers
Repetitive opening lines that subvert conventional wisdom:
- `I built [outcome] without [sacred industry ritual 1].`
- `I built [outcome] without [sacred industry ritual 2].`
- `I built [outcome] without [sacred industry ritual 3].`

### 2. Core Pivot
A sudden transition that compresses the solution to its fundamental primitives:
- `Because the only things required to achieve [outcome] are:`
- `1. [Primitive step 1]`
- `2. [Primitive step 2]`
- `3. [Primitive step 3]`

### 3. Reframe Closer
A 1-line statement that eliminates complex hacks:
- `There is no shortcut for it.`

---

## Quality Checklist

- Are the first two lines contrarian and readable in social feed view?
- Does every list line use an identical stem?
- Are there exactly 3 numbered steps?
- Is there zero fluff, zero em dashes, and zero emoji spam?
